Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Decision Maker ("the App") is a simple spinning wheel app built for personal and classroom use. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is handled.

Data We Collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter (option names, saved preset lists) is stored locally on your device only and never leaves it.

Analytics & Advertising

The App does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking pixels.

In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional $0.99 one-time purchase to unlock premium features. This transaction is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment information. Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies govern these transactions.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for use by children (including classroom use). Because no data is collected or transmitted, there are no special considerations for users under 13.

Data Storage

Any preset lists you save are stored on your device using its local storage mechanism. Uninstalling the App removes all stored data.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
